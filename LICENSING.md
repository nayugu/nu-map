## 9. Matters reserved for determination by the Licensor

*This §9 is a record of internal maintainer action items. It is not a term of
either license option and confers no rights.*

9.1. **Provenance of the requirements implementation — RESOLVED.**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) *Resolved:* the graduatenu **data** dependency is
fully retired. The `external/graduatenu` submodule was removed in July 2026,
program requirements are regenerated by the Licensor's own scrapers
(`scripts/scrape-majors.js`), and the residual references in `src/data/` and
`src/adapters/` are historical comments describing paths no longer used.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) *The former exposure.* The validation core of
`src/core/gradRequirements.js` — `checkReq`, `checkSection`, and `validateMajor`,
approximately 135 of the file's then 866 lines — had been written with reference
to graduatenu's `major2-validation.ts`, which is licensed under AGPL-3.0, to an
extent the authors did not recall. Were it a translation, a translation is
expressly a derivative work under 17 U.S.C. §101. The remaining lines, being the
allocation pass from `allocateMajor` downward, have no counterpart in graduatenu
and were never in question.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) *Why it required curing at all.* The position was
compliant under Option A and required no action there: the Work is itself
AGPL-3.0 and the attribution was recorded. It was **not** curable under Option B.
The AGPL confers no power to sublicense another author's material under
proprietary terms; a recipient's license runs from the original author (AGPL
§10), and §7 forbids the imposition of further terms. Compliance with the AGPL is
therefore precisely what permits distribution and precisely what withholds the
power to sell an exception.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) *Remedy effected.* The three functions
were reimplemented from a specification,
[`docs/major2-validation-spec.md`](docs/major2-validation-spec.md), derived only
from (i) the requirement vocabulary enumerated by machine across the Licensor's
own program data, a data format being a functional specification rather than
protectable expression, (ii) the Licensor's own test suite, and (iii) the result
contract required by the Licensor's own consumers, `src/ui/GradPanel.jsx` and
`scripts/lib/major-integrity.js`, that contract having no counterpart upstream.
`major2-validation.ts` was not consulted in producing either the specification or
the implementation. The implementation adopts its own decomposition — a
null-prototype dispatch table over module-level helpers, in place of a switch
with nested closures.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(e) *Equivalence.* The replacement was verified
differentially rather than by test suite alone: results are identical across all
1,006 programs in the shipped data by five placed-set variants each (29,310
requirement sections), and across all 1,192 distinct requirement-node shapes
occurring in that data, with the allocation pass confirmed unperturbed. The
15-case unit suite, the major-integrity check, and the build all pass unchanged.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(f) *Alternatives considered and rejected.* Permission
from the graduatenu copyright holders was unlikely to be obtainable: graduatenu is
a multi-contributor student project, and Sandbox would have had to secure its own
contributors' consent to relicense — the identical problem, at greater scale. An
opinion of counsel would have cost more than the rewrite and left the file in
place.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(g) *Residual position, stated with its limits.* The
Licensor's view is that the three functions as they now stand are its own
expression and may be conveyed under Option B. Three qualifications are recorded
rather than glossed:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(i) **The cure is prospective
only.** Versions of the Work already distributed carry the previous
implementation, and the version-control history retains it. This is immaterial
under Option A. It means, however, that a license under Option B must be granted
in respect of a version at or after the reimplementation, and that the Covered
Version recorded under `COMMERCIAL.md` §5.2(a) must be one such version. Option B
cannot be granted over the historical tree.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(ii) **A specification-based
reimplementation cures the problem only insofar as the specification itself
carried no protected expression.** `docs/major2-validation-spec.md` was written
from the requirement vocabulary as machine-enumerated from our own data, from our
own tests, and from the result contract our own consumers require; but the author
of the specification had read the previous implementation while establishing that
output contract, and did not work from the schema in isolation. The
Licensor's position is that what passed through is the schema's functional
vocabulary and our own result shape, neither being protectable expression of
graduatenu's. That is a reasoned position, not a certainty.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(iii) The Major2 *vocabulary*
remains graduatenu's contribution. The attribution at §6 and in the file header
is retained accordingly, that vocabulary being a functional specification which
attribution acknowledges as a matter of courtesy and record rather than of
obligation.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(h) **Nothing in this paragraph affects Option A, which is
what is presently in use.** The Work is AGPL-3.0, graduatenu is AGPL-3.0, and the
attribution is recorded. This paragraph bears on Option B alone.
