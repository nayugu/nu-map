Current undergraduate students apply to enroll in the PlusOne program.
With the PlusOne program, students attain their bachelor’s degree followed by a PlusOne year to complete the master’s degree.
Benefits Accelerate learning – get your master’s in one year vs.
typically two Reduce costs – apply some courses toward both your undergrad and master’s degrees, and get the MS for PlusOne tuition scholarship Gain a competitive edge – stand out with an advanced degree Below are the eligible undergraduate majors available to pursue a PlusOne pathway with a master’s degree in Advanced and Intelligent Manufacturing.
