Home / Programs & Admissions PlusOne Pathway Start earning your master’s degree early with the PlusOne pathway With an interdisciplinary focus, CAMD’s PlusOne pathway provides students a framework to earn undergraduate and master’s degrees in different subject areas.
The result is a highly flexible, practical and personalized academic experience that primes students for success in a wide array of emerging fields.
The PlusOne pathway enables students to:
Accelerate learning – Earn up to 16 credits toward a master’s degree during undergrad and receive your graduate degree up to one year sooner Gain more professional experience – A key part of the CAMD experience is co-op, or co-operative education.
With PlusOne at CAMD, students can complete up to three co-ops , giving students the professional experience in different fields and discover the connections between them, ultimately helping them to launch their careers.
Eligible Graduate Programs MS in Experience Design Experience Design is a holistic and integrative approach to design that focuses on the quality of the human experience in concrete situations.
It employs investigation, analysis, creativity and technology as tools to understand human goals, needs and desires, and to mediate the human experience.
The Experience Design program embraces research-driven design thinking for entrepreneurship and innovation, focusing on important global challenges of today.
More about Experience Design Download the Pathway Guide MS in Game Science and Design The Game Science and Design program that seeks to give students a comprehensive understanding of how successful game products are created in a player-centric environment.
Join an accomplished program and thriving academic community where you will develop a deep understanding of the playability and analytics that make products successful in an increasingly competitive marketplace.
More about Game Science and Design Download the Pathway Guide MS in Information Design and Data Visualization The Information Design and Data Visualization (IDDV) program equips students with the skills to transform complex data into meaningful visual narratives.
Bridging design, statistics and emerging technologies, the program prepares students to communicate insights clearly and ethically across a wide range of domains from public policy and healthcare to business intelligence and journalism.
With a focus on both creative exploration and analytical rigor, student s learn to craft data-driven stories that inform, persuade and inspire action in an increasingly data-rich world.
More about IDDV Download the Pathway Guide MA in Journalism The Journalism program merges fundamentals of journalism with training in cutting-edge digital tools and techniques.
More about Journalism Download the Pathway Guide MS in Media Advocacy Uniquely designed through a collaboration between CAMD and the School of Law, Media Advocacy is an interdisciplinary program focused on the intersection of communication, digital media, law and policy.
It is designed to teach strategic advocacy skills and prepare graduates to succeed as resilient, media-empowered citizens in a global society.
More about Media Advocacy Download the Pathway Guide MS in Media Innovation and Data Communication The Media Innovation and Data Communication program focuses on the current revolution in data storytelling and computational methods in media work;
the rapid evolution in video, animation, and augmented/virtual reality technologies;
and social networks and digital analytics.
Graduates will be prepared to become leaders in media firms and outlets engaged with cutting-edge technologies and innovative digital startups, as well as a broad range of media and communications organizations across the rapidly evolving digital economy.
More about Media Innovation and Data Communication Download the Pathway Guide MS in Media, Technology, and Ethics The Media, Technology, and Ethics program combines analysis, problem-solving, and hands-on projects that empower students to navigate the complex intersections of technology, media, and ethics.
Students will graduate with the intellectual concepts and practical skills to excel in diverse career paths in diverse industries including information technology, media, healthcare, or advanced academic research.
More About Media, Technology, and Ethics Download the Pathway Guide Master of Architecture Our NAAB accredited, STEM-designated MArch program takes an approach of radical pragmatism – finding design innovation within the realities of today’s urban challenges, from climate change and social equity to real estate markets and building preservation.
With Boston’s dynamic metropolitan area as your laboratory, students develop both the technical expertise and strategic vision needed to move architects to the center of discussions about our cities’ futures.
The MArch PlusOne pathway is available to CAMD BS in Architecture students.
Please contact the graduate program coordinator, Professor Marie Adams , to learn more about the PlusOne pathway and MArch 1-year program options.
More about M.Arch PlusOne Process Planning and Eligibility In order to be eligible to apply for the PlusOne pathway, you must have a minimum overall 3.000 GPA and have at least 64 semester-hours of earned or earned and in-progress credits .
Work with your primary academic advisor to determine if the PlusOne pathway will fit within your undergraduate program requirements and reach out to the graduate program coordinator to learn more about your program of interest.
Application Process Once you have completed the steps above, you can submit the PlusOne Inquiry Form to initiate the application process.
After submitting the inquiry form, you will receive an email with the link to the PlusOne pathway application form.
Within the online application, you will be prompted to upload the following materials:
Plan of Study indicating how the PlusOne courses will fit within your remaining undergraduate semesters Unofficial copy of your Northeastern transcript Resume Personal statement detailing your interest in the CAMD graduate program.
Please use your statement as an opportunity to explain your goals and how the program will help you achieve them.
Personal statements are generally 500–1000 words.
Contact information for three academic or professional references * Applicants for the MS in Experience Design, MS in Information Design and Data Visualization and MS in Game Science and Design programs may also be asked to submit work samples/portfolio materials.
Please note that the application fee and standardized testing are not required.
*Letters of recommendation are waived for applicants with a cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.300 and above.
If your home college is not CAMD, please also complete Parts 1 and 2 of the PlusOne Transition to Master’s form with your primary academic advisor and email the signed form to Cathy Bright, Associate Director of Graduate Operations, at [email protected] .
Other Options for Graduate Study at CAMD If the PlusOne pathway is not right for you, consider applying to begin a CAMD graduate program upon the completion of your undergraduate degree and take advantage of the Double Husky Scholarship .
Please note that students entering a CAMD graduate program via the PlusOne pathway are not eligible for the Double Husky Scholarship.
