Jumpstart Your Graduate Education Accelerate your IT education with the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology/Master of Science in Computer Science PlusOne program.
Work towards a master’s degree while still completing your bachelor’s degree and earn up to 16 credits that count towards both degrees.

Advance your career and gain seniority According to Burning Glass Labor Insights , 24% of computer science employers prefer hiring workers with advanced degrees over workers with only a bachelor’s degree.
Earn 18% more over your lifetime A Master of Science degree in Computer Science is a strong investment in your future – and it leads to higher salaries .
