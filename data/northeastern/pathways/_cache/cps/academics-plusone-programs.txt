Search PlusOne Programs The College of Professional Studies’ PlusOne Accelerated Master’s programs help you get ahead, so you can start earning your master’s degree while still pursuing your undergraduate education.
You’re one step closer to your goals Earn up to 16 credits towards your master’s degree while still working on your undergraduate degree so you can graduate one year sooner than you would with a traditional master’s program.
Save now and invest in your future The credits you earn count towards your master’s degree at no additional cost to you – so you save on tuition costs while you prepare to take the next big step in your career.
Gain a competitive edge In 2024, earning a master’s degree in the U.S.
typically results in a median salary premium of $6,000–$10,000 per year over a bachelor’s degree.
PlusOne programs give you a competitive edge in the labor market so you can get ahead.
