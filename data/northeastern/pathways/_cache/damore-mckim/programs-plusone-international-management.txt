Program details Our PlusOne MS in International Management program is designed for ambitious, academically outstanding students who want to accelerate their career in the field.
The program allows you to take up to 15 graduate credits during your senior year and apply those credits toward both your bachelor's and master's degrees.

Dive Deeper PlusOne advantages Faculty & learning Careers & support Alumni network PlusOne advantages Northeastern's PlusOne program lets you take up to 15 graduate credits during your senior year while still enrolled as an undergraduate.
These credits are double-counted toward both your bachelor's and master's degrees—saving you time and money.
You'll pay the lower undergraduate tuition rate for these graduate courses, then complete your remaining master's coursework in a single post-bachelor's term as a graduate student.

Application steps Discuss your plans with an academic advisor to ensure all prerequisites have been satisfied.
Create a new account for PlusOne in International Management by completing the PlusOne Inquiry Form .
You will receive an email with a link to the PlusOne application;
further instructions will be provided.
Once your application is underway, select “Program – PlusOne in International Management, Entry Term – Fall”.
Complete the application and submit when ready.
