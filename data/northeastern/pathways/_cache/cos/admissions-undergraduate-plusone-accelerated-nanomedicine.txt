View the FAQs Curriculum Requirements A maximum of 16 semester hours (SH) completed as an undergraduate can be used toward the Master of Science degree.
A fixed series of courses taken as an undergraduate replaces some of the required courses and general electives taken for the Bachelor of Science degree.
Check the course catalog for course descriptions.
Courses to complete as an undergraduate student NNMD 5270 Foundations in Nanomedicine:
Therapeutics (3 SH) NNMD 5271 Foundations in Nanomedicine:
Diagnostics (3 SH) NNMD 5370 Nanomedicine Research Techniques (4SH) PHSC 5560 Nanotoxicology (3 SH) NNMD 5570 Preclinical & Clinical Study Design (3 SH) NNMD courses are typically offered in Fall and Spring semesters only.
Some graduate courses may be completed during the summer depending on course availability.
Courses to complete as a graduate student Once you complete the undergraduate degree you become a full-time graduate student, enrolling in the same courses (18 credits) as other second year graduate students in the MS in Nanomedicine program .
A minimum of 34 graduate credits are required to complete Master of Science degree.
