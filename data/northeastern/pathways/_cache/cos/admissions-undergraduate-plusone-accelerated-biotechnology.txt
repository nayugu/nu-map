Explore Explore PlusOne and Accelerated Programs MS Biotechnology PlusOne The MS in Biotechnology program prepares students with the biotechnology expertise and professional skills they need for success in industrial, governmental, and clinical settings.
Students enrolled in the PlusOne program can accelerate their education by applying graduate-level coursework toward both their undergraduate and master’s degrees—earning both degrees in less time.
