Scroll To Overview PlusOne Programs Accelerated PhD Programs FAQs Select Section Overview PlusOne Programs Accelerated PhD Programs FAQs Overview Students enrolled in a PlusOne program can accelerate their education by applying graduate-level coursework toward both their undergraduate and master’s degrees.
This streamlined path allows them to earn both degrees in less time than it would take to complete each separately.
In most programs, students who maintain strong academic progress typically earn their master’s degree just one year after finishing their undergraduate studies.

Accelerated PhD Programs Students who are interested in advanced entry to the Biology or Physics Accelerated PhD programs should review the application requirements below and apply online.
Undergraduate transcripts will be automatically uploaded by Graduate Student Services once the application is complete.
No GRE or application fee is required.
Biology PhD Accelerated Program Students who are pursuing a BS in biology are eligible to apply for accelerated admission into the Biology PhD program.

What are the benefits?
Count up to 17 eligible undergraduate credits toward a master’s degree Save up to 50% on graduate tuition Save time—complete BS and MS degrees in five years Complete a graduate co-op What are the criteria to apply?
What are the criteria to apply?
Including but not limited to:
Cumulative GPA 3.0 At least two semesters left in undergraduate plan of study (three academic semesters prior for Marine Biology) When should I apply?
When should I apply?
Apply to enter no earlier than your fifth academic semester and at least two semesters before expecting to earn your Bachelor of Science degree (three academic semesters prior for Marine Biology).
What is the application process?
What is the application process?
Apply online starting in July.
Include individual plan of study.
No GRE, application fee, or transcripts required.
Is financing or scholarships applicable?
Is financing or scholarships applicable?
Any financial aid a student may have received from the university while an undergraduate will not continue once the student obtains graduate status.
PlusOne students are not eligible for the Double Husky Scholarship.
