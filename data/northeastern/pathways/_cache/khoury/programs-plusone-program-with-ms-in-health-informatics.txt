PlusOne Program with MS in Health Informatics In our PlusOne Accelerated Master’s Program, you can start earning your MS in Health Informatics during your undergraduate studies.
As you build your foundation in programming, data, and technology, you can take up to four graduate-level courses and apply them to both your undergraduate and graduate degrees.
This reduces the time to complete your master’s from two years to one, giving you a head start toward a fulfilling tech-driven career in health care.
