About NU Map

NU Map is an independent, student-built course planner. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Northeastern University.

Northeastern already has the authoritative tools. Banner is the source of truth for registration and current course information. The degree audit is the source of truth for your requirements and what you've completed. NU Map does not replace either of them, and doesn't try to.

What it replaces is the spreadsheet. The audit looks back and Banner handles right now; planning the semesters ahead of you was always left to you, usually in Excel. NU Map is that spreadsheet, rebuilt: it knows the catalog, the prerequisites, the offering history, and your co-op pattern. Plan here, register on Banner, verify with the audit and your advisor.

Why we built it

Planning meant coordinating dozens of spreadsheets and hundreds of webpages: the catalog for descriptions, Banner for offerings, the audit for requirements, another site for professors. The data all exists, but you have to log in every time and click into each course one by one. Just collecting everything into one list took hours.

And even then, say you get every class you need lined up. Now try putting them in order. You can't see the prerequisites! And even if you work out the ordering, you don't know whether a course actually runs in the spring, or whether it fills up. Then co-op scrambles the whole thing, because your semesters don't simply alternate the way sample plans assume.

NU Map shows the prereq chains, checks the plan as you build it, shows when each course actually ran and how full it got, and treats co-op blocks as part of the plan. Trying a what-if is another plan instead of a new spreadsheet, and a finished plan can go to a roommate or an advising meeting as a link, a short code, or a PDF.

Principles

NU Map trusts you. Real degrees are full of special circumstances — substitutions, placed-out courses, transfer credit, things your advisor approved that no tool could know about — so it lets you do basically anything. It warns you when something looks off, but it doesn't stop you.

Your plan stays in your browser. Nothing you enter touches a server; there is no account and no tracking. The privacy policy has the details.

The code and the data are open, so anyone can check our work. It is hand-translated into eight languages. And it is our principle to keep it free — so it is.

The story

NU Map is built by two students at Northeastern who plan more than is probably healthy. Before this existed, we did it the manual way: going through every past semester one at a time to figure out when courses actually run, and keeping it all lined up in spreadsheets. Eventually we built the tool we wished we had.
