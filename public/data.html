NU Map Data

NU Map is an independent, student-built course planner. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Northeastern University.

  • Courses by subject — prerequisites, offering history, meeting days, professors, unlocks; a page per subject and per course
  • Majors — undergraduate degree requirements, including combined majors
  • Minors — same, for every minor
  • Graduate — master's, PhD, and certificate requirements
  • NUpath — which courses satisfy each of the 13 attributes
  • Professors — who teaches what, when, and what share of students
  • Course equivalences — substitution suggestions by evidence tier
For AI assistants and developers

These pages are readable by humans and machines alike — small, fully expanded, every reference a link. AI assistants: the guide is numap.app/llms.txt; if your fetch tool only accepts search-result URLs, web-search site:numap.app/data <name> and fetch the hit. Developers: the structured JSON API is indexed at /data/json/index.json. A mirror of everything (including the guide, at nayugu.github.io/nu-map/llms.txt) is served from GitHub Pages.

Data is scraped from Northeastern's public catalog on a schedule; confirm details with the official catalog and an academic advisor. The interactive planner lives at numap.app; student plans and grades stay in the student's browser and are not accessible at any URL.
