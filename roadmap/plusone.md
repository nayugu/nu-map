# Planning a PlusOne (accelerated master's)

Planning a bachelor's and master's together, including which graduate courses can count toward both degrees.

We built a version, but there is no single reliable source for the rules. Each college publishes its own PlusOne pathways on separate web pages and PDFs, in different formats, and some of them are out of date. We could only keep a handful of roughly 75 pathways accurate, and a plan built on rules that might be wrong is worse than none. For now, your college's PlusOne page and your advisor are the place to check.
