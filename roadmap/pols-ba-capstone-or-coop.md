# Political Science BA: a co-op can replace the experiential course

The [catalog's](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/undergraduate/social-sciences-humanities/political-science/political-science-ba/#programrequirementstext) Experiential Learning Requirement is one course (POLS 4910, 4915, 4918 or 4942) or a co-op or study abroad. NU Map shows the course list as its own requirement, so if you do a co-op instead, that row stays unmet. You can ignore it.
