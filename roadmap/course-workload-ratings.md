# How much work a course really is, from students who took it

Roughly how many hours a week a course took and how hard it felt, pooled from other students and shown only as anonymous totals. The [privacy policy](/privacy.html) already spells out how it will work, so you can read the terms before it exists.

It only works once enough students share their answers, so we're not sure when it could start. Until then, a course's details link to its RateMyHusky reviews when there are any.
